GPT-5.6 in ChatGPT
Learn how GPT-5.6 Sol works in ChatGPT, which options are available by plan, and how usage limits and availability work.
Updated: 2 hours ago
GPT‑5.6 is gradually rolling out to eligible ChatGPT plans. If you do not see GPT‑5.6 Sol in the ChatGPT model picker, it may not be available for your account yet.
To access GPT‑5.6 in Codex, use one of these minimum versions:
ChatGPT desktop app (Codex mode): 26.707.30751
Codex CLI: 0.144.0
Overview
GPT-5.6 Sol is designed for complex work across coding, knowledge work and research, cybersecurity, science, computer use, and design. GPT-5.6 Sol Pro is the highest-capability GPT-5.6 option for difficult tasks and longer-running workflows.
GPT-5.6 Sol and a new reasoning slider are rolling out to eligible paid plans. GPT-5.6 Luna is becoming the default model for Free and Go users. GPT-5.6 Sol powers Instant, Medium, High, and Extra High on eligible paid plans, while GPT-5.6 Sol Pro powers Pro.
How GPT-5.6 works in ChatGPT
On eligible paid plans, ChatGPT can automatically use more reasoning for complex requests while Instant remains selected. To turn automatic reasoning on or off, go to Settings > General and adjust Higher intelligence.
You can also choose a reasoning level directly from the model picker. The options available depend on your plan and, for managed workspaces, your workspace settings.
Choose a speed and reasoning level
On eligible paid plans, use the reasoning slider to choose how much thought GPT-5.6 Sol puts into each response. Available options depend on your plan.
Free and Go users can use Think for harder questions. Think uses GPT-5.6 Luna, not GPT-5.6 Sol.
Enterprise customers may continue to see the existing model picker until the new controls reach their workspace.
Instant — Fast responses for everyday questions, powered by GPT-5.6 Sol on eligible paid plans and GPT-5.6 Luna on Free and Go.
Medium — Standard reasoning with GPT-5.6 Sol.
High — Extended reasoning with GPT-5.6 Sol.
Extra High — The highest reasoning effort available with GPT-5.6 Sol.
Pro — GPT-5.6 Sol Pro for difficult tasks and longer-running workflows.

Regional availability
GPT-5.6 does not have a separate country list. ChatGPT access follows the ChatGPT supported countries and territories, and OpenAI API access follows the OpenAI API supported countries and territories.
Eligible users in supported European locations, including the EEA, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom, and in the UAE can use GPT-5.6 when it is available for their plan and product.
Country availability and inference residency are separate. GPT-5.6 is not currently supported for workloads configured to use UAE inference residency. This does not prevent eligible users located in the UAE from using GPT-5.6 without that residency configuration.
For more information, see Data residency and inference residency for ChatGPT and API data residency.
Usage limits
GPT-5.6 uses the existing ChatGPT limits for automatic reasoning, manually selected reasoning, and Pro. Limits depend on your plan and, for managed workspaces, workspace settings.
Automatic reasoning does not count toward the usage allowance for manually selected reasoning.
Instant limits
Free and Go users have unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse-prevention safeguards. Separate limits continue to apply to file uploads, image generation, voice, data analysis, and other tools.
Reasoning usage and fallbacks
Manually choosing Medium, High, or Extra High uses GPT-5.6 Sol. If you reach a GPT-5.6 reasoning limit, ChatGPT may continue with another available reasoning model.
If you are on a Go plan, select Think to reason through harder questions. Think uses GPT-5.6 Luna, not GPT-5.6 Sol.
ChatGPT displays when an allowance resets when that information is available. You can use another available model or wait until the displayed reset time.
Managed workspaces and the ChatGPT Pro plan
For current managed-workspace limits and credit rates, see ChatGPT Business - Models and Limits, ChatGPT Enterprise and Edu - Models and Limits, and the ChatGPT Rate Card.
On the ChatGPT Pro plan, some models have separate usage allowances. When you reach a model's allowance, that model may be temporarily unavailable until the allowance resets. Reaching an allowance does not by itself mean that your account was restricted.
For more information, see About ChatGPT Pro tiers.
Usage must follow the Terms of Use. OpenAI uses safeguards to help prevent misuse, which may occasionally result in a temporary usage restriction. ChatGPT notifies you if this happens.
Safeguards
GPT-5.6 includes protections trained into the model, along with real-time checks and monitoring. Some higher-risk biological and cybersecurity requests may be refused or require additional checks. These safeguards can occasionally affect legitimate requests.
FAQ
Why do I not see GPT-5.6 Sol?
Confirm that GPT-5.6 Sol is included with your plan and that you are signed in to the correct account. If you use a managed workspace, ask an admin whether the model is available for your workspace or role.
Free and Go plans do not include GPT-5.6 Sol. These plans receive GPT-5.6 Luna.
Does GPT-5.6 replace GPT-5.5 Instant?
GPT-5.6 Sol is rolling out as the updated ChatGPT experience for eligible paid users. Free and Go users receive GPT-5.6 Luna. Earlier models may remain available where supported.
GPT-5.6 Sol powers Instant and the reasoning options on eligible paid plans, and GPT-5.6 Sol Pro powers Pro where available.
Can I select GPT-5.6 Terra or GPT-5.6 Luna in ChatGPT?
Terra and Luna are not selectable in standard ChatGPT conversations. Depending on your plan, they are available in Work and Codex. Developers can also use them through the OpenAI API.
Does GPT-5.6 change my ChatGPT usage limits?
Free and Go users have unlimited everyday text chats. File uploads, image generation, voice, data analysis, and other tools remain subject to separate limits.
Reasoning limits depend on your plan. For managed workspaces, see the plan-specific Models and Limits article or the ChatGPT Rate Card for current limits and credit rates.
Can Support reset my usage limits?
No. OpenAI Support does not reset ChatGPT or Codex usage limits. If you reach a limit, wait until it resets or use another available option shown in your account.
If you believe your usage was counted incorrectly, or access has not returned after the stated reset time, contact Support so we can investigate.
